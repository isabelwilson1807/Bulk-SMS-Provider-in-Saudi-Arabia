## Promotional, Transactional, and OTP Messaging

Different SMS categories serve different business purposes.

**Promotional SMS:** Used to communicate special offers, discounts, product launches, and marketing campaigns to customers who have consented to receive them.

**Transactional SMS:** Used for important updates related to customer activities, including payment confirmations, order information, and service notifications.

**OTP SMS:** Used to verify user identity during registration, login, and other authentication processes.

Selecting the right message category helps businesses organize their communication effectively.
